# BETCRAFT DESIGN SYSTEM

## Brand Vision

BetCraft is not a printing shop.

BetCraft is a premium production studio where ideas become physical products.

The experience should feel like the intersection of:

* A luxury print house
* A modern creative agency
* A high-end product manufacturer
* A premium design studio

The website must communicate craftsmanship, precision, creativity and trust.

Every section should feel intentional and visually memorable.

Avoid generic SaaS layouts.
Avoid template-like sections.
Avoid excessive minimalism.
Avoid empty whitespace without purpose.

The design should feel custom-made.

---

# Creative Direction

Visual inspiration:

* Luxury packaging studios
* Modern print ateliers
* Premium editorial brands
* Design agencies
* Product showcase websites

Keywords:

Elegant.
Bold.
Crafted.
Tactile.
Premium.
Professional.
Creative.
Modern.

The website should feel expensive without relying on gold colors or flashy effects.

---

# Visual Identity

## Atmosphere

The interface should feel like high-quality paper meets modern technology.

Use visual depth inspired by:

* Paper textures
* Print layers
* Ink overlays
* Embossed details
* Premium packaging

The goal is subtle sophistication.

Never look corporate.
Never look generic.
Never look like a dashboard.

# Typography

Primary Font

Inter

Display Font

Playfair Display

Typography hierarchy is critical.

Headlines should feel prestigious.

Body text should feel modern and highly readable.

Large headlines may use Playfair Display.

Supporting text should use Inter.

Avoid oversized typography that dominates the viewport.

---

# Layout Philosophy

Every section should feel unique.

Do not stack identical blocks.

Create visual rhythm through alternating compositions.

Examples:

* Left image / right content
* Full-width showcase
* Split layouts
* Layered cards
* Overlapping elements
* Editorial compositions

The page should feel curated rather than assembled.

---

# Hero Section

Create an unforgettable first impression.

Large product photography.

Showcase:

* Apparel
* Promotional products
* Signage
* Branding materials
* Custom printing

Use layered compositions.

Combine:

* Real products
* Floating design elements
* Large typography

Headline should communicate transformation:

"We Turn Ideas Into Premium Products"

or similar messaging.

Include two CTAs:

Primary:
Request a Quote

Secondary:
View Portfolio

---

# Portfolio Section

This is the most important section.

Do not use boring cards.

Create a dynamic showcase.

Use:

* Masonry layouts
* Large visual blocks
* Asymmetrical grids
* Hover interactions

Products should feel tangible and desirable.

---

# Services Section

Present services as premium capabilities.

Examples:

* Sublimation
* DTF Printing
* Embroidery
* Promotional Products
* Branding Materials
* Graphic Design

Each service should have:

* Visual icon
* Short description
* Elegant interaction

---

# Process Section

Use a luxury timeline.

01 Concept

02 Design

03 Production

04 Delivery

The process should feel smooth and professional.

---

# Testimonials

Use large customer quotes.

Prioritize authenticity.

Avoid generic testimonial cards.

Create editorial layouts with photography.

---

# Call To Action

The final section should feel powerful.

Large headline.

Strong visual background.

Clear CTA.

Create urgency without appearing aggressive.

---

# Components

## Buttons

Elegant.

Solid primary buttons.

Subtle hover animations.

No gradients.

No glowing effects.

## Cards

Large radius.

Generous spacing.

Premium shadows.

Use depth sparingly.

## Images

Large.

High quality.

Editorial.

Products must be the visual hero.

Never use tiny thumbnails.

---

# Animations

Subtle and premium.

Use:

* Fade
* Reveal
* Scale
* Parallax
* Smooth transitions

Avoid:

* Bounces
* Excessive motion
* Gaming-style effects

Animations should feel expensive.

---

# AI Generation Rules

DO NOT generate:

* Generic SaaS layouts
* Startup templates
* Dashboard aesthetics
* Empty minimalist sections
* Oversized whitespace
* Repetitive cards
* Boring grids

DO generate:

* Unique layouts
* Premium visual compositions
* Creative asymmetry
* Large product showcases
* Elegant storytelling
* High conversion design
* Memorable brand presence

The final website should look like a premium creative production studio capable of serving major brands.
